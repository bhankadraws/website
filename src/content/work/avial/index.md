Avial is my MDes thesis project from the Glasgow School of Art.

Avial is a 28-page dialogue-less comic that weaves in and out of the lives of a few people living in a building complex in India, while another character makes the dish Avial\*. Punctuated only by diegetic sounds, it is an exploration of connection, loss, and societal pressure in a South Asian context and how to find universality in the specific. It is also supplemented by an audio file that gives you a soundscape while reading the book. It debuted at the [Thoughtbubble comic convention](https://www.thoughtbubblefestival.com/debutingbooks) in 2024 and was featured in a review in [Broken Frontier](https://www.brokenfrontier.com/avial-bhavani-balasubramanyam/).

\*Avial (n) – A South Indian thick stew of different vegetables commonly found in the Western Ghats, seasoned with coconut oil and curry leaves. See the GSA Showcase [here](https://gsashowcase.net/school-of-design/mdes-communication-design/). Read the full comic [here](https://issuu.com/bhankadraws/docs/avial_issuu).
