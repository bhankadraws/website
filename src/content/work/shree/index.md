Shree was the outcome of a 3-month long project for an elective at GSA – Worlding Fictions and Fictional Worlds. The brief was to create a portfolio based on the idea of "fictioning" – exploring real-world problems through the analysis of fiction through a feminist and decolonised lens. I created a visual development portfolio for a prospective animated film, set in an alternative future where India had not been colonised by the British Empire.

It is a work in progress, but below is the portfolio I submitted as the final outcome for the elective.
