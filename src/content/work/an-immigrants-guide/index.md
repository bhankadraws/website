_An Immigrant's Guide to Infiltrating a Place_ is a zine that interrogates UK immigration rhetoric through archival media, illustration, and satire. Centred around a pigeon as a metaphor for migrants welcomed for their utility and rejected when they are no longer convenient, the work exposes how colonial attitudes toward belonging and movement persist in contemporary Britain. It is available for purchase in [Goodpress Glasgow](https://goodpress.co.uk/products/an-immigrants-guide-to-infiltrating-a-place-by-bhavani-balasubramanyam?_pos=16&_sid=520da2464&_ss=r), and is in various libraries across the UK, like the [Baltic Archive](https://archive.baltic.art/object/bhavani-bala-an-immigrants-guide-to-infiltrating-a-place-ma3609).
