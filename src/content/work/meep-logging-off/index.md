Meep is a story about depression, the isolation that can often accompany it, and the difficulty one faces in asking for help. This is a one-page zine that folds out into a print, done for Thoughtbubble 2024. I also created an accompanying risograph print based on the final spread. It was recently reviewed by Andy Oliver in [Broken Frontier](https://www.brokenfrontier.com/meep-logging-off_-bhavani-bala/).
